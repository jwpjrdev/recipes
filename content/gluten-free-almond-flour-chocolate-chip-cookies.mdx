## Concise

## Ingredients

- `1/4` cup (`53`g) brown sugar
- `1/2` teaspoon baking soda
- `1/4` teaspoon salt
- `2` tablespoon unsalted butter, softened
- `2` teaspoon vanilla extract
- `1/8` teaspoon almond extract, optional
- `1` egg
- `2` cup (`192`g) almond flour
- `1/2` cup (`85`g) chocolate chips

<Tip>
For sweeter cookies, increase brown sugar to `1/2` cup (`106`g) and increase salt to `1/2` teaspoon.
</Tip>

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 350°F.
2. In a large bowl, combine brown sugar, baking soda, salt, and butter, and mix until smooth.
3. Mix in the vanilla, almond extract, egg, and almond flour, stirring to combine. Scrape the bottom and sides of the bowl and mix for 1 minute at medium speed.
4. Stir in the chocolate chips.
5. Use a small cookie scoop to scoop dough onto a baking sheet, about 1 1/2" apart—they won't spread much.
6. Gently flatten the cookies to about 3/8" thick, about 2" diameter.
7. Bake for 9-12 minutes, until their edges are starting to gently brown. Do not overbake or they will be dry.
8. Cool cookies on the pan for 2 minutes before transferring to a wire rack to cool completely.
