## Concise

## Ingredients

- `1` cup (`198`g) vegetable oil
- `2 2/3` cup (`528`g) granulated sugar
- `4` egg
- `1` 15oz can (`425`g) pumpkin purée
- `2/3` cup (`152`g) water
- `3 1/3` cup (`400`g) all-purpose flour
- `1/2` teaspoon baking powder
- `1` teaspoon baking soda
- `1 1/2` teaspoon salt
- `1` teaspoon nutmeg
- `1` teaspoon vanilla
- `1 1/2` cup (`255`g) chocolate chips (optional)
- `1` cup (`113`g) chopped walnuts or pecans (optional)
- coarse white sugar, for sprinkling on top (optional)

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 350°F. Line two loaf pans with parchment paper.
2. In a large bowl, beat together the oil, sugar, eggs, pumpkin, and water.
3. Add the flour, baking powder, baking soda, salt, nutmeg, and vanilla, stirring to combine.
4. Mix in the chips and nuts, if you're using them.
5. Spoon the batter into the prepared pans. Sprinkle the tops of the loaves with coarse sugar, if desired.
6. Bake for 60-80 minutes, or until a cake tester or toothpick inserted in the center of the loaf comes out clean; and that same tester inserted about 1/2" into the top of the loaf doesn't encounter any totally unbaked batter.
7. Cool bread on a rack. When it's completely cool, wrap in plastic wrap and store overnight before serving.
