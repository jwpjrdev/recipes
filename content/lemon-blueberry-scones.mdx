title: 'Lemon Blueberry Scones'
category: 'scones'

## Concise

## Ingredients

### Scones

- `2` cup (`250`g) all-purpose flour
- `6` tablespoon (`75`g) granulated sugar
- `1` tablespoon lemon zest
- `2 1/2` teaspoon baking powder
- `1/2` teaspoon salt
- `8` tablespoon (`1` stick) unsalted butter, frozen, cut into 1/2 tablespoon slices
- `1/2` cup heavy cream, cold
- `1` egg, cold
- `1 1/2` teaspoon vanilla
- `1` heaping cup (`140`g) blueberries, fresh or frozen

### Topping

- `1` tablespoon water
- `1` tablespoon heavy cream, cold
- coarse white sugar, for sprinkling on top (optional)

### Glaze

- `1` cup (`120`g) confectioners' sugar
- `3` tablespoon fresh lemon juice

## Instructions

1. In a large bowl, whisk together the flour, sugar, lemon zest, baking powder, and salt.
2. Add the butter to the flour mixture and work with your hands until the mixture comes together into pea-sized crumbs. Place the bowl in the freezer.
3. In a small bowl or liquid measuring cup, whisk together `1/2` cup of the heavy cream, egg, and vanilla. Remove the flour mixture from the freezer. Drizzle the cream mixture over the flour mixture and add the blueberries. Gently mix together with a spatula or wooden spoon until everything appears moistened and the ingredients are just combined.
4. Lightly flour a work surface. Pour the crumbly mixture onto the surface and, with floured hands, work the dough into a ball as best you can. The dough should be sticky and shaggy. If it's too sticky, sprinkle a little more flour on top.
5. Divide the dough in half and form into `2` discs. Use a sharp knife or a bench scraper to divide each disc into 6 wedges.
6. In a small bowl, mix `1` tablespoon heavy cream with the water, then brush it on the scones. Optionally sprinkle coarse sugar on top.
7. Place the scones on a plate or a parchment paper-lined baking sheet and refrigerate for at least 15 minutes or up to 24 hours. (If refrigerating for more than an hour, lightly cover the scones.)
8. Preheat oven to 400°F. Line a large baking sheet with parchment paper. Arrange the scones 2-3 inches apart on the baking sheet.
9. Bake for 22-25 minutes, or until lightly browned on top and golden around the edges. Cool the scones on the baking sheet for 5 minutes before glazing.
10. Sift the confectioners' sugar into a small bowl or liquid measuring cup, and mix in lemon juice. Drizzle the glaze over the scones. Optionally glaze a second time.

<Tip>
You can freeze scones instead of refrigerating, and when ready to bake, bake straight from frozen and add a few minutes onto the baking time.
</Tip>
