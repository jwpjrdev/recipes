## Concise

## Ingredients

- `2` cups rolled (old-fashioned) oats
- `1/2` cup whole almonds (toasted then coarsely chopped)
- `1/2` cup all bran cereal
- `3` tablespoons butter
- `2/3` cup honey
- `1/4` cup brown sugar
- `1` teaspoon vanilla
- `1` pinch salt
- `3/4` cup dark chocolate chips, chopped

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 350°F. Line a 9x9 pan with parchment paper. Use a larger pan if you want thinner bars.
2. To toast almonds, spread onto a baking sheet and place into 350°F oven. Bake 5-8 minutes or until they are golden brown. Remove immediately from the pan to cool before chopping.
3. In the bowl of your food processor add the oats, almonds (coarsely chop before adding), and all bran, and pulse until the almonds are in smaller pieces (you do not want powder).
4. In a saucepan, combine the butter, honey, brown sugar, vanilla and salt, and bring the mixture to a boil. Stir, then take off the heat and let cool for approximately 5 minutes.
5. Pour the dry ingredients into a large bowl and then pour the honey mixture over it. Let cool 8-10 minutes before stirring in the chocolate chips.
6. Press the mixture into the prepared pan, sprinkling some chocolate chips over the top if desired.
7. Bake for 20-25 minutes. Let cool completely before cutting.
