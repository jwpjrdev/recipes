## Detailed

## Ingredients

- `24` tablespoon (`3` stick) unsalted butter, softened
- `1 1/4` cup (`240`g) granulated sugar
- `1 1/4` cup (`240`g) brown sugar, packed
- `1` tablespoon vanilla
- `2` egg
- `2` teaspoon baking soda
- `1 1/2` teaspoon salt
- `4` cup (`480`g) all-purpose flour
- `4` cup (`24`oz) chocolate chips or Andes mint chips

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 350°F.
2. In a stand mixer, beat butter, sugars, vanilla, and eggs on medium speed until light and fluffy.
3. Stir in baking soda and salt until thoroughly mixed, then add flour and stir until thick. Stir in chocolate chips.
4. Use a small cookie scoop to scoop dough onto a baking sheet, 2 inches apart.
5. Bake for 11-13 minutes or until light brown (centers will be soft). Cool for 1-2 minutes and then transfer cookies from the baking sheet to a cooling rack.
